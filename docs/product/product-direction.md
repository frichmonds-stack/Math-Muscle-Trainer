# Product Direction

Last updated: 2026-07-11

## Product Purpose

`Math Muscle Trainer` is a strength-themed arithmetic practice app for building fluency, confidence, and visible growth across addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

The app should make regular arithmetic practice feel purposeful and motivating without depending on noisy gamification.

## Target Learners

- primary students who need clear touch-friendly arithmetic practice
- older learners who still benefit from guided fluency and mastery structure
- adults who should not feel the app is childish or patronizing

## Training And Mastery Philosophy

- Regular use should come from useful skill-building, learning habits, visible growth, and mastery.
- Practice and Learn should share one underlying prompt-response-feedback logic even when the visible UI differs.
- Mastery should be evidence-based and should help learners decide what to train next.
- Adaptive learning should be app intelligence by default, not a blunt learner-facing on/off switch.

## Pedagogy Principles

- The user is the pedagogy source of truth for lesson wording and teaching moves.
- Minimize extraneous cognitive load so attention stays on arithmetic.
- Prefer short, clear steps and visible progress gates over dense explanation dumps.
- Preserve room for worked examples, hints, scaffolds, guided practice, and final practice inside Learn.

## Product Shape

- Home is a daily training dashboard.
- Workout is the configurable practice surface.
- Learn is guided technique-building.
- Progress is evidence and growth, not judgment.
- Debug mode is teacher/developer tooling only.

## Experience Direction

- iPad-first, especially landscape classroom use
- all-ages visual tone: strong, calm, tactile, and readable
- minimalism as a learning strategy, not just a style preference
- responsive support for portrait/mobile, but not phone-first product planning

## Accessibility Intent

- touch-friendly controls with generous targets
- readable contrast in dark and light modes
- visible keyboard focus and non-color-only state meaning
- learner-friendly cognitive load and predictable navigation

## Commercial Direction

- A subscription-based future remains plausible, but it is not implemented.
- Any future work involving accounts, cloud sync, analytics, payments, student identifiers, or networked feedback requires a dedicated privacy/security review first.

## Major Enduring Constraints

- Keep the app static and dependency-light unless the user explicitly chooses a bigger platform shift.
- Do not invent pedagogy when the user has not provided it.
- Preserve saved-data compatibility through normalization and migration-aware changes.
